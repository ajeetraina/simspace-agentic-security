This is the whole Product Catalog stack, and it's where vulnerabilities actually enter. On the left - **without the sandbox, the agent picks base images freely**: the application on `node:20` brings 2 Critical and 26 High, PostgreSQL adds 8 High-plus, Kafka on `confluentinc/cp-kafka:7.6` piles on 12 High-plus, aws-sdk drags in more, and the **total stack exposure is 2 Critical and 46-plus High CVEs**. On the right - **with the sandbox plus the DHI MCP, the agent queries first** and every service resolves to a hardened image: `dhi.io/node`, `dhi.io/postgresql`, `dhi.io/kafka`. Watch the right column collapse to **0 across the board - total stack exposure 0 Critical, 0 High**. The point isn't just that DHI is cleaner; it's that when the agent has to *ask* before it picks, the vulnerabilities never enter the stack in the first place. That prevention-at-the-source is the whole idea, and next we make those attestations tangible.
