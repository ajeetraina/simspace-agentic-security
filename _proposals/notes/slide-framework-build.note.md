Four-questions framework as a 4-frame spotlight build (slide-framework-1..4.webp),
replacing the single slide-framework.webp. Each frame keeps one card at full blue and
fades the other three toward white. Generated by _proposals/gen_framework_build.py from
the raster assets/slide-framework.webp (the pasted blue-card image). Presenter arrows
through them to highlight each question in turn.

1. **Evidence** - No matter how the agent produced a change, governing it comes down to **four questions**. One - Evidence: what is actually in this artifact, and where did it come from? That is **SBOM, VEX, and SLSA provenance** - the audit trail that lets you answer "who approved that build?"
2. **Baseline** - Two - Baseline: did it start from something trustworthy? A **Docker Hardened Image** instead of whatever the agent grabbed off the internet. Evidence tells you what you have; baseline makes sure you began from a good place.
3. **Gate** - Three - Gate: is it allowed to pass? **Build policies, image signing, and admission** - the check in the middle of the pipeline that fails closed.
4. **Boundary** - Four - Boundary: what could it reach while it worked? The **sandbox runtime** - network, filesystem, and credentials. Evidence and baseline make governance possible; gate and boundary make it real. The next slide draws these four as a single dev-to-prod road.
