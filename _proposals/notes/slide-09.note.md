This is our ungoverned baseline - the **agent without a sandbox**, and it's exactly how a lot of teams are running today. Look at the container: the agent is sitting right on **your host**, with the host daemon, host credentials, and no boundary at all. We hand it a simple **prompt** - "containerize this app" - and because it has **full permissions** and can reach **open registries with no allowlist**, it just grabs whatever it wants. What it picks is `FROM node:20`, chosen with **no guidance**. And the result, down at the bottom, is the number we're going to keep coming back to: **0 critical, 6 high, 30 medium, 54 low CVEs** - built from **431 packages**, with **no SBOM, no attestation, running as root**.
